Welcome to Intokozo Yemfundo Project

Bringing joy to education

Intokozo Yemfundo Project was established in the year 2016 by females who had passion to pass on the knowledge to other disadvantaged township learners

In 2022 Intokozo Yemfundo Project hosted a spelling bee competition where 4 different township schools competed in the spelling bee. Children won different prices but only one school won a big prize which was a sum of money

The goal of the spelling bee was to help children with learning how to spell and improve their vocabulary as some children cannot spell or have a problem when it comes to English

The goal of Intokozo Yemfundo Project is instilling an attitude of excellence and achievement early in the learning career. We aim to enrich and empower young minds that hold the future.
